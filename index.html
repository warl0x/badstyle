Lead Photographer & Creative Force · Orange County, CA

Welcome to Badstyle Photography

I’m Adam, the lead photographer and creative force behind Badstyle Photography. Based right here in Orange County, I specialize in crafting bold, cinematic, and deeply personal boudoir portraits that celebrate exactly who you are.

My approach to photography is rooted in a dynamic visual style—drawing inspiration from vivid graphic art, edgy street aesthetics, and striking editorial portraiture. I don’t just want to take a standard photo; I want to create a standout piece of art that makes you feel powerful, confident, and unapologetically yourself.

Session Atmosphere

The Experience

Stepping in front of a camera for a boudoir session can feel intimidating, which is why creating a laid-back, zero-pressure environment is my absolute top priority. My goal is to make the entire process feel less like a nerve-wracking photoshoot and more like a collaborative creative project.

From getting the lighting dialed in perfectly to finding those natural, unposed moments with a classic 85mm portrait lens, I guide you through every single step. You don't need to know how to pose or have any modeling experience—directing the flow of the shoot to capture your best angles is what I’m here for.

Artistic Vision

The Philosophy

Boudoir isn’t just about the final gallery of images; it’s about the transformative experience of owning your space and seeing yourself through a new, artistic lens. Whether you’re marking a personal milestone, creating a bold gift for a partner, or simply celebrating your own unique edge, I’m here to capture it with raw authenticity and serious style.
